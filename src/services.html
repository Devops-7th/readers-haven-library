What We Offer

🔎 Online Catalogue

Search every title, check availability and reserve books from home.

Available 24/7. Steps: search, select a book, click Reserve.

📱 E-Books

Borrow e-books and audiobooks on your phone or tablet with your member ID.

Available 24/7. Up to 3 e-books for 14 days.

🪑 Study-Room Booking

Reserve a quiet room for group work or exam preparation.

Daily 10:00 AM - 6:00 PM. Book at the desk, 2 hours per slot.

🖨️ Printing & Scanning

Black-and-white and colour printing, plus free document scanning.

Mon - Sat, 9:30 AM - 5:30 PM. PKR 5 per printed page.

📶 Free Wi-Fi

Fast, free internet throughout the library for all members.

Open hours. Ask the desk for the daily password.
